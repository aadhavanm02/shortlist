Open the web app Get the Android app

What it does

Daily matches

Jobs from LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and company pages, ranked by how well they fit your titles, cities, salary and skills.

Honest tailoring

AI suggests changes you accept or reject. Skills you don't have become questions, never fake lines.

One-page PDF

A clean, ATS-friendly resume that always fits one page, plus a record of every job you applied to.

How it works

Pick your profile

Upload your resume once and set the jobs you want.

Open a match

Read the job and tap Tailor resume to this job.

Download and apply

Accept the changes you like, save the PDF, and mark it applied.
